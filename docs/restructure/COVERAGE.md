## What the plan cannot promise

- **"100%"**: the plan was checked against the code, the live site and an independent review, and every request above
  has a step. It is still a plan: each step proves itself with a test that fails first, and the owner checks the live
  result at seven stops (README «Order»). Most of those stops (✋P0, ✋Q1, ✋N1, ✋C2, ✋M1, ✋W1, ✋A2) were NOT run as
  recorded checks; he switched the phases on himself instead.
- **Telegram's name, logo, artwork and sounds** are not copied (`telegram-parity.md`): the layout and behaviour are.
- **Other systems** (the orders system, the media buyer) are never edited from this repository: the plan writes one
  ordered prompt per repository.
- **Nothing here was seen on a real phone or measured in a browser.** The build machine could not start a browser
  or a phone: every claim about speed, feel, sound and the look on a phone rests on tests and on reading the code.
- **The Android app (M1b) is not built.** The web side is what runs inside it; its native chat, call window and
  topic-call ring are still the old ones. It cannot be built or tried from here.
- **Accurate live transcription and fast voice answers wait for his graphics card.** Until then the meeting minutes
  arrive after the call and the words are only as good as the processor's recogniser.
- **The orders system's products wait for HIS address and token.** Nobody else types them.
- **The installed site's `start_url` waits for his answer on D-N1** (what the app opens on).
- **Most decisions of `DECISIONS.md` are still «بانتظار المالك»:** each was built with the recommended default and
  can change when he answers; a pending decision never blocked a phase.
