# S3.2 — «لوحة التحكم» as its own place with ONE navigation, and «الناس» as ONE section (`settingsOneDoor`, OFF)

## Definition of Done
- What must work (all of it only while the switch `settingsOneDoor` AND `controlPanelV2` are on, `panelOneDoorOn`; OFF is today, byte for byte):
  1. **Its own place.** An ADMIN or OWNER who holds a section of the panel (S3.1's `oneDoorHidesAdmin`) gets «لوحة التحكم» in the slot «الإدارة» had: ONE row to `/owner`, no tab strip and no «المزيد» (`oneDoorPanelPlace` in `src/lib/nav/places.ts`), on the old and the new map. It keeps the place key `admin`, so the saved menu order, the app bar and `locate` (every address of the administration marks it) stay as they are. A lead keeps «الإدارة» with its tabs, a member keeps «الناس» (directory + «لوحتي»), a guest is untouched.
  2. **ONE navigation.** `PanelFrame` with `oneNav` draws six entries and no other list: نظرة عامة · الناس · التقارير · الهيكل والأداء · الأرشيف والرقابة · مساعد المالك (`PANEL_ONE_NAV`, names in `cpone.nav.*`). The list is `panelSectionsFor`'s, re-grouped by `panelOneNavFor` (the capability guard stays exactly the panel's: an admin gets «الناس» and «التقارير», a team lead the overview, the people and the reports, a member nothing, a guest «not found»). The section titles wear the navigation's name (the structure section is «الهيكل والأداء» everywhere on that page).
  3. **«الناس» is ONE section** (`PeopleOneSection`) with three parts, each the existing body MOVED: `accounts` «الحسابات والصلاحيات» (`OrgMembersView`: the person cards, and in the shared person card/sheet the rank and every permission), `templates` «قوالب الصلاحيات» (`TemplatesPart`, the owner rank's editor, now one function shared with the old section), `activity` «النشاط» (the board's people table, `PeopleSection` with a `part`). Stable ids: `/owner?s=people&part=accounts|templates|activity` (`permissions` leads to `accounts`; no part = the first one the person may open; a part they may not open is «not found»; one part = no part strip, `PartNav` otherwise).
  4. **The settings sections are not in the panel.** `/owner?s=spaces|features|integrations|company` lead into «إعدادات الشركة» of the settings frame (`/settings/me/<key>`, the part and the query ride along), `?s=accounts` leads to the part `accounts`, `?v=people|reports|ai` to its new place. A person who holds only settings sections is sent to the first of them, never to an empty panel.
  5. **Old addresses.** `/members`, `/analytics`, `/structure`, `/development`, `/archive`, `/owner/assistant` open their place in the one navigation (`/members?new=person` → `/owner?s=people&part=accounts&new=person`); `/spaces`, `/automation`, `/telegram`, `/owner/assistant/settings` open «إعدادات الشركة» (`oneDoorHref`, `panelRedirectFor` is the one door every page asks, with the same capability guard as before: a person who may not open the target keeps the page the address always was).
  6. **The person card stays ONE component** (`members/member-card.tsx` + `member-sheet.tsx`, untouched): S5.5 puts the verification control there.
- Tests: `tests/control-panel-people.test.ts` (new, 33), `tests/db/control-panel-people.test.ts` (new, 19).
- Edge cases that matter: the door without the panel; an admin whose boxes were taken away (only «المساحات», no entry: sent to the settings frame); a partner without `archive.view`; a team lead (activity only); a person with only the accounts (one part, no strip); the walk of the ten old addresses follows every redirect and the target answers.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · no schema, no migration.
