The owner decided «لا بس كتابة»: **no audio is ever stored, uploaded or kept anywhere.** Only written lines are kept. Builds on S6.1 (already on `main`).

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **The switch** `meetingMinutes` (group «المالك», depends on `assistantInMeetings`, OFF by default; name, hint with the ⚠️ / 🔧 notes, `FEATURE_META`, `ar.meeting-minutes.json`). It works only while `assistantInMeetings` and `ownerAssistant` are on too (`minutesSwitchesOn`).
  2. **The owner turns it on for ONE call** from the call room: «سجّل النص» / «وقّف تسجيل النص» (`POST /api/calls/[id]/minutes {on}`, OWNER only; anybody else gets «not found»). The voice agent is the only thing that can write the words, so turning it on invites the assistant into the room (or tells it, when it is already there) and puts `transcript: true` in the signed join. If the agent cannot be reached the record is left OFF (a mark that records nothing would be a lie). Sending the assistant out turns the record off. Audit lines `call.transcript_on` / `call.transcript_off` (call id only, never a word).
  3. **A visible mark for everybody:** `src/components/calls/recording-mark.tsx` draws «عم يتسجّل النص» (with the days in its tooltip) for every person who can join the call, mounted once in `call-room.tsx`. It asks `GET /api/calls/[id]/minutes` every 20 s and on `call.changed`. The owner also gets the two buttons and a small «⚙️» panel with the retention days.
  4. **The signed door** `POST /api/voice-agent/transcript` (beside `/turn`, the SAME HMAC: `x-agent-timestamp` + `x-agent-signature` over `<time>.<body>`, fresh within 60 s) takes only `{callId, personId, text}` (any other field, an `audio` one for example, is dropped). Unsigned, wrong secret, stale, **replayed** (a signature is good once) or no secret on the server: a plain 404. After that: the line is stored only for a LIVE call with the record ON, switches on, for a person of that company, at most 1500 characters (422 over), at most 120 a minute per call (429). When the record is not on it answers `{stored:false}` (not an error) and the agent stops sending for 60 s.
  5. **The agent** (`voice-agent/agent.py`): while `transcript` is on it POSTs each finished, non-noise sentence with the speaker's id to the door (the address is derived from `WORKSPACE_TURN_URL`, or `WORKSPACE_TRANSCRIPT_URL`); a new signed `/transcript` handler flips it when the owner switches it mid-call. It writes no audio file anywhere (pinned by a test that reads the source).
  6. **Retention:** `CallTranscriptLine` rows older than the company's days (`Organization.settings.meetingMinutes.retentionDays`, default 30, 1..365, set by the owner from the call room, audited as `org.meeting_retention`) are HARD-deleted by the job `callTranscripts` (`src/server/jobs/call-transcripts.ts`, in `runDueJobs`, last). It runs even when the switch is off. A deleted person takes their lines with them (cascade).
  7. **The minutes after the call:** `endCall` and `finishCall` (the two places a call ends) call `queueMinutes` (one line each, never throws): a call that had the record on (or has lines) becomes `minutesState = pending`. The job (`src/server/calls/minutes.ts`) builds, through the owner's own provider (`resolveProvider(..., "owner")`, his quota and usage): the lines go through `buildChatPrompt` (phones, e-mails, links, secrets and addresses masked; people as «زميل n» unless the owner allowed names; the meeting title through `redactTitle`); the model is asked for JSON `{summary, decisions[], tasks[]}`, parsed defensively (a non-JSON answer becomes the summary, never a task). Real names are put back only on the server, in what the owner reads. A failing model is retried every 15 minutes for a day, then `failed` (the lines stay). While the switch is off the call just waits.
  8. **Where it goes:** the assistant's line (the same `{k:"assistant"}` line «@المساعد» writes, with the cards under it) in the call's conversation; a meeting by link has none, so the space's «عام» topic; else nowhere and the minutes are only on the call and at `/calls/[id]/minutes`. A conversation with a guest among its people, a closed or archived one: no line (same rule as the assistant's call line).
  9. **Tasks are PROPOSAL CARDS, never created by themselves:** each proposed task becomes an `AiProposal` (`create_task`, PENDING) in a new `AiConversation` of the owner (the same table, routes and checks as «مساعد المالك»; the assignee only when the speaker is staff; the project is the space's first ordinary project; none without one). The cards show under the line (for the owner only) and on the minutes page. A test approves one and sees the task made by the ordinary service, and counts that no task exists before.
  10. **Who reads:** the OWNER (minutes, cards, and on his page the full written text) and the person who started the call **when staff** (the minutes only, no cards, no text). Everybody else, every guest and a company with the switch off: «not found». No route sends a raw line (pinned by a test on the route source); the only place a line is read is the owner's own page.
  11. **The promise:** `ar.assistant-meetings.json` hint now says «ما بينحفظ أي صوت أبدًا» and that with «تسجيل نص الاجتماعات» only TEXT is kept; `calls.minutes.promise` («بينحفظ النص بس، مش الصوت أبدًا، لمدة {days} يوم») is drawn in the owner's panel; the guide article `calls` (`articles.ts`) says the same.

## Honest limits (tell the owner)
- **Without a graphics card the minutes arrive AFTER the call, not live.** The speech is written on the processor (slow and not accurate in Arabic: README), the job runs every 30 seconds and asks the owner's model once the call has ended. The UI says so (the page, the owner's panel, the switch's hint).
- **The words are as good as the speech recogniser:** a wrong word on the processor is a wrong word in the minutes. The assistant's own spoken answers are not in the written lines (the agent does not listen to itself); they are in the conversation as text lines already (S6.1).
- **The lines are stored as said** (they are the company's own words, in the company's own database, deleted after the days): what is masked is only what leaves for the model. The full text is shown to the owner on his page only.
- **The minutes line goes to everybody who reads the call's conversation** (or the space's «عام» topic for a meeting by link): that is the spec, but it means people who were not in the meeting can read the summary and the decisions. If the conversation has a guest in it, nothing is posted and the minutes stay on the call.
- **A card expires after 48 hours** like every proposal of the owner's assistant.
- A long meeting: the model reads the newest part that fits (600 lines or 30000 characters for a big model, much less for a small local one). Map-reduce minutes for long meetings belong to 6.3 (after the card).
- Python could not be run here (no python on this machine): the agent's change is read and pinned by a source test only; `voice-agent/test_logic.py` is unchanged (nothing in `logic.py` changed). Please try one real call.
- Not run here: the full unit and database suites, a browser (no dev server), the real voice agent and calls server.
