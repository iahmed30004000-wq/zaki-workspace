# P0.5 — The privacy regression matrix

## Definition of Done
- What must work: ONE table-driven database test says who sees what, and every surface must agree with it. Personas (the
  primary owner; a partner with `archive.view`; a partner without it; an ADMIN of the company outside the space; the space's
  ADMIN; a member in the private things; a member of the space in none of them; a member of a «مقطع»; a guest) × objects
  (an open topic, a restricted topic, a «مقطع»'s topic, a DM; a public task, a «المعنيين بس» task, a task made from a
  private topic) × surfaces (a conversation: the door, the chat home / the oversight list, the search, the policy; a task:
  the door, the task list). The expected answers are DATA at the top of the file, readable by the owner.
- Tests: `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts` (new, 8 cases: 4 conversations, 3 tasks, the table's own shape). It adds no
  production code.
- Edge cases that matter: a DM of two others is listed only in the oversight list of the silent eye, so that list counts as
  the «listing» surface; a partner without `archive.view` is refused by the oversight list itself; every row has exactly one
  cell per persona (checked), so a missing cell cannot pass silently.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none).

## The failing test, before the change
A regression test passes on correct code by design, so it was proved by breaking the code on purpose: with the oversight rule
made rank-based again (the P0.1 bug: `hasOversight` returning `isOwnerOf(org)` alone) the matrix fails on the two rows that
matter and the change was then reverted:
```
× conversations > restricted: … agree with the table   (4 cells wrong: the partner without archive.view reads it)
× conversations > dm: … agree with the table           (3 cells wrong)
Tests  2 failed | 6 passed (8)
```
On the real code: 8 passed.

## What changed
- `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts` only. How a later step adds to it: a new kind of thing is one line in `CONVERSATIONS` or
  `TASKS` (nine cells, in the order of `PERSONAS`) plus its creation in `world()`; a new place where people or work show up
  is one more entry in the surfaces loop of the matching `describe`.
