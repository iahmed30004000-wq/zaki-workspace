# W1.4 — «الطلبات»: one inbox of everything that waits for a decision (`workRequests`, OFF)

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **One screen, two tabs.** `/requests` has «بانتظاري» (default) and «طلباتي» (`?tab=mine`). Sections of «بانتظاري»: **approvals** whose turn is mine (the same rows as `/approvals?v=waiting`); **task assignment requests** addressed to me (`TaskAssignRequest`: today it had NO list); **the open tickets of MY departments** (member or lead; nobody took them, or handed to me and not finished); **cards in «قيد المراجعة»** in a space I moderate; **product sheets** in review (Q1.1) for the owner and the approvers he named only. «طلباتي»: my approvals still open or sent back, my assignment requests, my open tickets, my cards in review, and the sheets I sent to review (for whoever works in the knowledge space).
  2. **Each row acts in place through the EXISTING doors, no new decision path:** approvals `POST /api/approvals/[id]/decide` (approve · ask for changes with a reason · reject with a reason); tasks `POST /api/tasks/[id]/request/accept|return` (return needs a reason; `requestId` is always sent); tickets `PATCH /api/tickets/[id]` («استلمها» = in progress + assigned to me, «حُلّت» = resolved); cards `PUT /api/cards/[id]/status` (approve · back to a draft); sheets `PUT /api/knowledge/[id]/status` (approve at the version that was seen · back to a draft). The server of each door decides again (rank, turn, rights): a refusal is shown under the row in Arabic. Nothing here loosens a rank rule: assigning to a higher rank is still a request, the request is still answered only by the addressed person or the owner. After an action the list and the number are refetched (TanStack `invalidateQueries`, no `router.refresh`).
  3. **Privacy: every row and every count is filtered by what the person may see.** Tasks: P0.2 `taskVisibleWhere` on the request's task (a request on a private task its addressee is no part of is neither listed nor counted); approvals: the spaces the person still works in (their step, their turn); tickets: departments the person is in + `tickets.view`; cards: spaces the person moderates (the owner everywhere), file cards only with `files.view`, and never a product sheet or a general answer (the cards door `loadCard` refuses those, they are the knowledge door's and appear only under «sheets»); a **guest (external partner) sees nothing** (403 on the route, the notice on the page, a count of 0). The numbers are the caller's own.
  4. **The badge on «الشغل»** = the count of «بانتظاري»: `GET /api/workspaces` carries `waiting` (the caller's own, 0 while the switch is off and for a guest), counted with the rows' own filters (counts only, no rows are read); `place-badge.tsx` has `useWorkWaiting()` (reads the shell's one spaces query: no second request) and the words for a screen reader.
  5. **`/approvals` keeps its pages** (list, a request's page, forms, form builder); «الطلبات» links into them (`/approvals?v=…`, `/approvals?new=1`, `/approvals/forms`, each row to `/approvals/[id]`).
  6. **The map.** The tab `requests` of the new map points at `/requests` (it was `/approvals`); `/approvals` stays an address of the same tab (`also`). It leaves the map only when BOTH `approvals` and `workRequests` are off (it holds more than approvals). With `workRequests` OFF (the default) `/requests` serves today's approvals page unchanged, so the new map's tab never dead-ends.
  7. **Switch** `workRequests`, default OFF (`features.ts`, the two Arabic lines, `tests/features.test.ts`). Off: the route answers 404 for everybody, no number on «الشغل», the tab is the approvals page.
