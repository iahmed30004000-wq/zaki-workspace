# W1.7 — «اليوم» simplified: at most six cards, one team card (rides on `navV2`)

## Definition of Done
- What must work (with the switch `navV2` ON; no switch of its own, no schema, no migration, no new door):
  1. **Up to six cards, in this order**, decided by ONE pure function (`todayLayout`, `src/lib/today.ts`): «مهامي اليوم» (today + overdue, the inline quick add at its foot — it lands in «صندوق المهام» through W1.2's `POST /api/instant/inbox` when `workInbox` is on, unchanged) · «بانتظار قرارك» (W1.4's count and its newest five rows, only while `workRequests` is on and only when something waits) · «ذكروك» (unread mentions, one row per conversation with its count, each a link into that conversation; only when there is one) · «تذكيراتي» (the existing reminders card, with Q1.5's `followUpAt` listed under it as «مواعيد اليوم») · «التسجيل اليومي» (only while `checkins` is on) · ONE team card for the owner and whoever holds `users.manage`. A partner gets «مهامي اليوم» and nothing else of the six.
  2. **ONE team card**: the old «نبض الفريق» (open, overdue, the most overdue tasks and who holds them), the smart cards' «الفريق هالأسبوع» strip (finished this week, due today, people late) and the owner's whole panel on the home page were three team cards; they are now one — the numbers of the first two in one card (`teamCardOf`), the most overdue list folded inside it (`details`), the panel's own page («لوحة التحكم», `/owner`) linked from it when the person may open it (`controlPanelAccess`). The **scope chip is always visible** («المساحة: X» inside a space — what the page counts — «كل الشركة» / «المساحات المتاحة لك» outside one).
  3. **The rest moved, nothing was deleted**: the personal ring and numbers, the catch-up digest, «وقتي اليوم», the pinned tasks and the active projects sit under «اليوم › المزيد» (one folded native `details`, closed by default, drawn only when something is inside). `TODAY_HAND_OFF` + `placeOfPiece` + `OLD_TODAY_PIECES` are the tested hand-off table: every part of the old page is a card, in «المزيد», a guide strip, or handed off (the owner's panel → `/owner`).
  4. **Privacy**: all task data through `taskVisibleWhere` (the existing `getToday` / `getTodayDashboard`); «بانتظار قرارك» takes its rows and count from `workRequestsOf` (P0.2 for requests on tasks, the approver's turn, departments, spaces moderated); «ذكروك» takes its numbers from `chatList` (the chat list's own unread rule, `canRead` has the last word, a conversation the person has no row in counts nothing — the owner's silent oversight adds nothing and nothing is written; the text of a message is never read); «مواعيد اليوم» reads only the person's OWN unfinished tickets and only their titles, behind the tickets «يشوف» box, never the customer's number. A partner gets none of these.
  5. **With `navV2` OFF the page is the old one** (render test: no card slot, no «المزيد», the owner's pulse and the member's dashboard as before; every old test of the page is unchanged and green).
  6. **The «اليوم» strip** keeps `ThemeDecor kind="strip"` (calm in the new flat themes by F1.7, D1): pinned by a source test; none of the new parts animates.

No owner decision is pending. Choices inside the plan's words, to confirm in the owner's check ✋W1:
