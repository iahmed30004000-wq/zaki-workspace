  - `/spaces/[id]/settings` is ONE page with tabs «عام · الناس · المقاطع · الدردشة · الشغل · خطر» (`?tab=`; every tab is an address, the back button and a pasted link land on the same tab). The third tab keeps today's word «المقاطع» (D-N4, «الفرق», is not answered): the label is ONE string, `spset.tab.teams`.
  - A person sees only the tabs they can use. The space's manager (`isSpaceManager`, untouched: D-A2 is open) gets «عام، الناس، المقاطع، الدردشة، الشغل»; «خطر» is added only for whoever can really archive (OWNER and ADMIN rank, as `setSpaceArchived`) or delete (OWNER, as `deleteSpaceWithWork`); never for «الشركة». A department's space is read-only here and points to «الأقسام».
  - «الدردشة» shows what is true today: the topics every space starts with («عام»، «التنبيهات») with who writes in each, and who opens a new topic (the owner and the space's admin). Shown, not editable (see "Not done"). No topic of the space is listed: a restricted topic stays private.

On `/projects` the buttons of the space become links to the page (the manager: «إعدادات المساحة» and «المقاطع»; a plain member: «أعضاء المساحة» and «المقاطع»); an external partner keeps the dialogs.
