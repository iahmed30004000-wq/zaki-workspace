# C3b.3 — Albums (one mosaic for 2 to 10 pictures) and the media viewer (`chatAlbums`, ON)

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **No schema, no migration, no new door, no new dependency.** `MessageAttachment` already stores `width` / `height` and `AttachmentDto` already carries them; the layout reads those.
  2. **ONE pure layout function** `src/lib/chat/album-layout.ts` (`layoutAlbum`): 1 full · 2 side by side · 3 one big over two · 4 a 2x2 of equal cells · 5 to 10 rows of 2 or 3 (`albumPlan`: 5 → 2+3, 6 → 3+3, 7 → 3+2+2, 8 → 3+3+2, 9 → 3+3+3, 10 → 3+3+2+2). Rows are justified (a row's cells take widths in proportion to their pictures' shapes, so a row has the height that fits them with no crop). Whole pixels, `x` counted from the inline start (the first picture sits at the start, the right side in Arabic), rows touch, every row spans the whole width, nothing leaves the box. The crop is bounded: shapes are laid out between 3:4 and 9:5, a row is held between 18 % and 75 % of the width (a lone row up to the width), and `cellCrop` is tested (ordinary photos lose at most 30 %, a very thin or very wide one at most 66 %); the viewer always shows the whole picture. A stack taller than 480 px is made NARROWER (same shapes), never squeezed. A picture with no size (or nonsense) takes the 4:3 fallback; more than ten are cut to ten.
  3. **The virtual list reserves the album's box.** `albumBox()` in `viewport/sizes.ts` is the layout's own answer (one picture: `imageBox`, unchanged); the bubble draws the same box with CSS `aspect-ratio`, so the estimate and the drawing agree. `estimateRowHeight(row, { albums: true })` reserves ONE album box instead of the stacked pictures. See «Not done here»: the option is opt-in.
  4. **The bubble** (`bubble-media.tsx`): two or more pictures or videos of our own addresses are one mosaic (cells are buttons placed by percentages with `inset-inline-start`, so it shrinks with a narrow bubble and flips in a right-to-left screen); a video cell is a muted still with a play mark (the native controls are the viewer's); a lone picture keeps its box, download line and «اقرا النص» and becomes a button; files, voice notes and audio keep their own lines; the Q1.2 knowledge gallery and the voice and file bubbles are not touched. A file that is not on our own address is never in the mosaic. A deleted message draws nothing (the bubble already did not).
  5. **The composer sends up to ten media in ONE message with ONE caption.** Verified (not changed): `MAX_ATTACHMENTS_PER_MESSAGE` is 10 on the client and the server and `chatc.too_many_files` says 10; the send uploads each file and posts ONE message with all the ids and the one body. Added: the tray now refuses the eleventh at PICK time (`takeRoom`, with the words «الحد الأقصى 10 مرفقات للرسالة» at once, not after the upload), the tray's ref moves at once so two picks in the same breath are counted together, and `FileTray` shows «n من 10 مرفقات، بتنبعت كلها برسالة وحدة بتعليق واحد» from two files on.
  6. **The media viewer** (`media-viewer.tsx`): full screen over everything (a real Radix dialog: focus kept inside and given back, the page behind inert and not scrolling), ← / → and swipe, wheel / pinch / double-tap zoom (+ − 0 on the keyboard, drag to move while zoomed), download, «اعرض بالمحادثة», the caption, native video controls, the position «n من m». Direction follows the screen: in a right-to-left screen the LEFT arrow and a finger moving to the right are NEXT (`stepOfKey`, `swipeStepOf`, pure and tested). **Esc closes the viewer and only the viewer**: Radix handles the key first (the event is then `defaultPrevented`, which `shortcutOf` ignores) and a `[role="dialog"]` is on the page (which `overlayIsOpen` counts), so C2.6's key layer does not also leave the conversation. The viewer's events do not reach the bubble it was opened from (a portal passes React events up: a double click would reply, a right click would open the menu, a long press the sheet): the content stops every pointer, touch, click, double-click, context-menu and key event.
  7. **ONE source of the list**: `viewerItemsOf(messages)` — the pictures and videos of the LOADED messages, oldest first, never the files of a deleted message, a voice note, a system or pending message, or an address that is not ours. It is recomputed while the viewer is open (a message deleted meanwhile drops out at once) and the viewer follows the picture by its id (an older page put in front moves nothing). The caption is the message's words through `stripFormatting` (a spoiler stays hidden). C3d swaps this one function (and the provider's `items`) for `GET …/media`.
  8. **Switch `chatAlbums`, ON** (`features.ts`, the two Arabic lines, `tests/features.test.ts`). OFF: `BubbleMedia albums={false}` is exactly the old rendering (one box per picture, no button, no viewer).
- Tests (new, all green): `tests/chat-album-layout.test.ts` (51: every count's shape, the tile with no hole and no overlap for 1 to 10 pictures across six shape mixes, crop bounds, fallback sizes, purity, the album's media rule, the list's reservation, the composer's ten, the viewer's list / deleted / direction / zoom arithmetic, the dialog and Esc chain, the events that must not reach the bubble, theme tokens and logical sides in the viewer source), appended to `tests/chat-v2-bubble-render.test.ts` (8: the mosaic and its box, start order, a video cell, a file beside the pictures, a lone picture, the switch OFF, a foreign address, a deleted message) and `tests/features.test.ts` (+1).

## Independent review
A read-only review of the staged diff (`/code-review`, medium; it ran no test) found nine things. Six are real and fixed, each with a source or behaviour pin in `chat-album-layout` that was added WITH the fix (not run red first: honest note, the fixes were small and the pins were written right after them):
1. **The wheel listener and the direction were read in mount effects**, but Radix draws a dialog's content one commit AFTER the component (its portal mounts in a layout effect; the test reads the installed Radix source), so the stage was null and the wheel zoom was dead and the direction stuck at right-to-left. Now callback refs (`setStage`, `setContent`).
2. **The viewer lived inside the bubble that opened it**: deleting that message, or the virtual list dropping the row while new messages arrived, took the open viewer with it. With a provider the viewer is now drawn above the list (`open` in the context); without one it is still the bubble's own.
3. **«اعرض بالمحادثة» then close gave focus back to the bubble it was opened from**, which scrolls it into view and undoes the jump. The close does not return focus after a jump.
4. **The estimate and the bubble disagreed about a foreign address** (the bubble dropped it before `albumMediaOf`, the estimate did not). `albumMediaOf` now owns the rule (`safeMediaUrl` moved to `album-layout.ts`), and both ask it.
5. **A right or middle button started a pan or a swipe** in the viewer. Only the primary mouse button, a finger or a pen is a gesture.
6. **`OCR_MIMES` was defined twice**: one list in the viewer, the bubble imports it.
Not taken: (a) the `chatAlbums` switch and the estimate's default are not wired because they are other packages' files; both are recorded in «Not done here» and the estimate's default is the pinned C1.7 behaviour on purpose; (b) `alt` on the cell pictures inside a labelled button: the button's children are presentational for a screen reader, so the name is not read twice.
