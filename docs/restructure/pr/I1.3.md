# I1.3 — Money stays in the app, never in chat: «طلب مصروف» and «تحويل بين الصناديق» (`expenseForms`, OFF)

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **Two templates on the EXISTING approvals module** (`FormTemplate` + `ApprovalRequest`, no new table, no migration): a form whose JSON schema carries `purpose: "expense" | "transfer"`. «طلب مصروف» = amount, currency, which box, reason (+ the invoice photo through the form's usual file picker); «تحويل بين الصناديق» = amount, currency, from box, to box, optional reason. The fixed fields (`amount` number, `currency` choice of the five Arabic currency names, `box` / `from_box` + `to_box` choice, `reason`) are all required and checked by the validator; the manager names the boxes and the chain. The builder offers both as a «ابدأ من نموذج جاهز» button on a new form (`presetEditor`), and an edit can never drop the purpose (`approvals.f.purpose_locked`).
  2. **The amount never travels in words.** `amount` + `currency` are lifted out of the answers into the request's own columns (integer minor units through `amountTextToMinor`; 0.3 is 30, three decimals and exponents are refused) and are kept out of `details` and out of the stored `answers`, the bell, the audit log (still only `hasAmount`) and the card. A transfer to the same box is refused at `to_box`.
  3. **Approved in «الطلبات»** through the one existing door (`POST /api/approvals/[id]/decide`, the chain, exactly once): nothing was added to the decision path. A request of a money form cannot be resent through the plain boxes (title, details, amount): only through its answers (`approvals.f.money_answers`).
  4. **The decision is a card message** in the space's «عام» topic (`systemKey: general`): kind `CARD`, `card = { type: "approval", requestId, outcome, purpose }`, a readable line («قرار: تمت الموافقة على «طلب مصروف» (الاسم)») that carries NO amount, no currency, no reason and no title typed by anybody (the kind comes from the purpose). Written for APPROVED and REJECTED only (not «اطلب تعديل», not a middle step of a chain). The decider writes it where the topic lets him post (the owner only where he joined; a closed or archived topic takes nothing); else the person who asked; else nothing, and the decision still stands. It never throws and never undoes a decision.
  5. **`cash.view` wired.** The amount of a money request is shown only to holders of `prices.view` or `cash.view` (read from the database; a guest holds none) and to the person who asked (their own). Cut on the server in `toListItems` (list, waiting view, the W1.4 inbox, a request's page), in the earlier versions (`previous[].amountMinor/currency`) and in the invoice photo (the attachment list and `mayReadApprovalFile`, so `/api/files/[id]/raw` answers 404). `ListItem.amountHidden` (present only on money requests) lets the screen say «المبلغ مخفي عنك» + 🔧. An approver without it sees the request, the reason and the box, not the amount. `cash.view` is in `WIRED_CAPABILITIES`; the OWNER holds it by rank, an ADMIN does not (only `prices.view`). A guest sees nothing (403 `guest.no_access`).
  6. **⚠️ + 🔧 on the form's screens** (`ExpenseNote`, in the builder and above the filler): internal office spending only; order money, wallets and cash balances stay in the orders system; no approval from a chat message; an approver without the capability sees no amount and no invoice.
  7. **Switch** `expenseForms`, default OFF, group «المهام والشغل», depends on `approvals`. Off: a money form cannot be made, edited or filled (404 `features.off`), the ones made are not listed or readable as forms, no card is posted; requests already made stay readable and their amounts stay cut (privacy never relaxes with a switch).
