## What changed
- `src/server/chat/list.ts` (new): `chatList`, `spaceTopicRows`, `rowsForSync`, `loadConversationBatch`, and `chatScope(ctx)`. ONE query returns, per conversation, the policy's facts, the viewer's own rows, the other person of a private chat and (with stats) the newest message (a deleted message's text is never selected), the unread and mention counts. The SQL narrows what is loaded with the old home's rules; `canRead` of `policy/conversation.ts` then has the last word on the rows it returned (`listable`), so the two can only disagree in the safe direction. A private chat is listed for its two people only (the silent eye reads other people's through the oversight list), a guest has topics only, an archived topic is its managers', somebody else's assistant chat is never listed.
- `chatScope(ctx)`: the F1.1 finding. `cache()` has no scope in a route handler, so the company's settings, the person's company facts and their box of capabilities are kept in a `WeakMap` keyed by the request's own `ctx` object (`activeOrgCtx()` makes a new one per request). Measured on the same open topic: the old detail reads `Organization.settings` 15 times, the header once; the list route once; sync not at all.
- `src/server/chat/topics.ts`: `conversationHeader`, `conversationMessages`, `messagesQuerySchema` added; `recallOffer` / `problemOffer` take an optional switch reader (default unchanged). `conversationDetail` is untouched. The header answers like the old detail for every person on every kind of conversation (a test compares them, `me.*` and the fields, for six people on four conversations with the Q1 switches on); the one deliberate difference is 404 instead of 403 for a guest asking for a private chat.
- `src/server/chat/sync.ts` (new): see below. `src/server/chat/space-topics.ts`: `spaceTopics`, `ensureStartersForViewer`, `ensureStartersForSpace` (writes only into a space that is the person's own; loads `list.ts` lazily so `workspaces.ts` does not pull the chat core). `src/server/chat/dto.ts`: the new shapes. `src/lib/chat/conversation/client.ts`: URL builders. Routes: `list`, `sync`, `conversations/[id]/header`, `conversations/[id]/messages` (GET beside the POST C1.8 edits), `spaces/[spaceId]/topics` (GET beside POST).
- Registries: `src/lib/features.ts` (`chatOneList`, default ON), `ar.features.json` (name and hint), `ar.chat-errors.json` (`chat.e.page_one_cursor`), `tests/features.test.ts`, `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts`.

**The sync feed.** Events of the company after `after`, in order, `limit` at most 500 (`more: true` when the answer stopped at the limit; the cursor is the last event looked at, even one that is not in the feed). Conversations of the batch: loaded and judged in ONE query; messages hydrated in one `findMany` with `conversationId IN (the readable ones)`. Marks: the current value for each pair an event names, ACTIVE people only, the owner's silent reading left out. Rows: the conversation's own row and the row of its space. `removed`: from `conversation.changed` and from `membership.changed` about the viewer, only for a conversation they could have known (a row of theirs, a private chat with their id in its key, a topic of a space they are in) or one that no longer exists. Not in it: `typing` (not even a row), `call.ring`, `call.changed`, `whiteboard.changed`, `watch.changed`. An event aimed at one other person is skipped; one aimed at me with no conversation (rank, permissions, mute) answers `reset`. Without `after` it only says where now is. `reset` also answers a cursor ahead of anything issued or behind what the purge kept (the answer's cursor is where now is, so there is no loop).

**Carried over from Q1** (one case each in `chat-sync.test.ts`, each also compared byte for byte with the same message through the page route): the product CARD (address only, no price anywhere in the answer, a guest gets the same address), the order CARD (the number only), the ticket chip (number, status, holder for staff, nothing for a guest; `ticket.changed` re-sends the message), «لازم ينقرأ» (total / read / missing for the author and moderator, `mine` / `acked` for a member, nothing for a guest, a ✅ moves the count). The header carries `canOrderState`, `canRecall`, `recallKinds` and `canMustRead` (cases in `chat-header.test.ts`).
