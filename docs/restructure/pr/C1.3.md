# C1.3 + F1.5 — The live line: one line per browser, one question per event, badges and presence from frames

## Definition of Done
- What must work: (1) the hub asks ONE question per event about who of the connected people may read the conversation (a lookup of at most four queries, however many people and tabs), remembers the answer per PERSON for 30 s, writes to every stream without asking anything per stream, writes the delivered ticks of a DM once per person per second after the loop, and `membership.changed` forgets the answers of THAT company only; (2) the tabs of one browser share ONE stream: the first holds the Web Lock `zk-rt` and owns the EventSource, re-broadcasts every frame on `BroadcastChannel("zk-rt")`, another tab takes over when it goes (from the last event id it heard, with a refetch), and a browser without Web Locks or BroadcastChannel opens one per tab exactly as before; (3) the F0 guarantees survive the new topology (below); (4) `presence.changed` reaches the people dock when somebody's first stream opens / last closes, as a stream-only nudge; (5) the unread badge, the space counts, the people dock and the quick chat refetch on frames, and the polls that fed them are gone (a five-minute safety refresh stays); (6) nobody receives a frame of a conversation they cannot read.
- The F0 traps, each pinned: **F0.5** the leader sends the OR of every tab's visibility under its own tab id, a follower never posts, a follower that closes or goes silent (75 s) is forgotten, and a hidden leader with a visible follower says «visible»; **F0.2** `evicted` and `signed_out` reach every tab, and two tabs of one browser open one stream so they cannot evict each other; **F0.3** the 25 s ping, the 60 s watchdog, the jitter and the 15 s stream-down polling are unchanged in the leader, a hidden leader keeps the stream while any tab is visible and pauses only when every tab has been hidden for 60 s (a follower being shown wakes it), and a follower that hears nothing from the leader for 60 s polls every 15 s; **F0.1 / reload** the leader's `reload`, resume and polling signals are sent to every tab, and a new leader resumes from the last id it heard.
- Tests: `tests/live-line-fanout.test.ts` (22), `tests/live-line-client.test.ts` (21, several tabs in one process with a fake Web Locks and BroadcastChannel), `tests/live-line-badges.test.ts` (15), `tests/db/realtime-live-gate.test.ts` (7: the batch is the SAME rule as `canReadConversation` for every kind of conversation and person, and a real fan-out), `tests/db/realtime-fanout-calls.test.ts` (3: the bill, counted), `tests/db/realtime-presence-frame.test.ts` (6, over the real `/api/rt`), `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts` (+7: the live gate for the four kinds of conversation, ids only, the presence nudge). Changed because they pinned a behaviour, not a rule: `tests/realtime-hub.test.ts` (the DM's delivered ticks are written a second later: `flushDeliveredForTests`), `tests/quick-chat.test.ts` (the 4 s poll is gone). Must stay green and did, unchanged: `tests/realtime-client-*.test.ts`, `realtime-review-fixes`, `realtime-one-registry`, `realtime-kinds`, `economy-ui`, `chat-unread-badge`, `people-dock`, `nav-shell-v2`, `tests/db/realtime-stream|notify|listener-cursor|review-fixes|replay-gate`, `chat-viewing-push`, `last-seen-presence`, `chat-live-events`, `presence-dock`.
- Edge cases that matter: two events of one conversation at the same instant share one lookup; a lookup that fails delivers to nobody and is not remembered; an answer asked before a `membership.changed` is not remembered after it; a reload (close then open inside 4 s) says nothing to the dock; an owner who hides himself (`ownerHiddenOnline`) is never announced, and a switch that cannot be read counts as «hidden»; a guest is never nudged and never announced; a stream that cannot be written to is dropped; a tab that stops listening before it was ever leader leaves the queue for the lock; a leader whose lock is lost (crash) is replaced.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none: no schema, no migration).

## What changed
- **Server.** `src/server/realtime/readers.ts` (new): `readersAmong(conversationId, people)` — the policy's own pure `canRead(facts, org)` over facts loaded for all of them at once (the conversation with its members, the people's company rows, space rows, team rows, and the DM's pair): 3 to 4 queries whatever the number of people. It is not a second rule: `tests/db/realtime-live-gate.test.ts` proves it answers exactly like `canReadConversation` for an open topic, a restricted topic, a «مقطع» topic, a DM, a DM whose other person left, a group, a guest, a suspended and an offboarded person, the silent eye with and without `archive.view`, an org admin outside the space, and somebody of another company. `policy-gate.ts`: `readersOf` (a test that stubs only `canReadConversation` still works, person by person). `hub.ts`: the per-stream cache is gone; answers are kept per company → conversation → person (30 s), questions in flight are shared, `membership.changed` forgets one company, `conversation.changed` one conversation, a conversation's kind is remembered (no read per message to know it is a DM), the DM's delivered ticks are queued and written once per person per second (`DELIVERED_DEBOUNCE_MS`) after the fan-out, `filterVisible` (the replay gate) asks once per conversation, `presence.changed` (below). A frame of a stream-only kind no longer carries an empty `id:` line (it reset the resume point). `conversation.read` frames carry `self: true` on the READER's own streams (so a badge ignores somebody else's read mark; ids only).
- **`presence.changed`.** Sent by the hub when a person's first stream opens and, after a 4 s grace (a reload or a hand-over between tabs reopens one at once and says nothing), when the last closes. A nudge: `data: {"kind":"presence.changed"}`, no `id:` line, no person, never stored, never replayed, not in `REALTIME_KINDS` (`RtEvent`'s kind union has it; `tests/realtime-kinds.test.ts` pins the one list). Only people who may see the roster get it (`seesPresence`: not a guest, holds `directory.view`), never the person it is about, never another company; the roster route (`/api/presence`) still applies space scoping and the hidden owner when the page asks again. `api/rt/route.ts` passes `role` and `seesPresence`.
- **Client.** `use-realtime.ts`: the leader / follower line (Web Lock + BroadcastChannel, messages `frame`, `alive`, `leader`, `hello`, `vis`, `gone`, `evicted`, `signed_out`), `subscribeRealtime`, refetch signals marked `synthetic` and sent to every tab, `presence.changed` delivered to the presence handlers only (a screen's `useRealtime` handler never hears it: the conversation screen and the chat list would refetch on it). The public `useRealtime`, `useTyping`, `useOnline`, `useStreamEvicted` are untouched. `live-refresh.ts` (new): `watchFrames` / `useFrameRefresh` (one refetch per burst, counted from the FIRST frame so a steady stream cannot starve it), `safetyRefreshMs()` (five minutes, through `pollMs`), `movesSpaceCounts`, `touchesConversation`.
- **Screens.** `use-chat-unread.ts` (frames, my own read mark, five-minute safety; somebody else's read mark no longer refetches), `people-dock.tsx` (presence frames), `space-switcher.tsx` (`useSpaces(sync)`: the shell's one reader listens to frames; `/api/workspaces` and its pinned shape are untouched), `quick-chat.tsx` (the 4 s and 20 s polls are gone: the window refetches on frames of its own conversation, the list on presence; it still reads the OLD detail route `GET /api/chat/conversations/[id]`), `notification-bell.tsx` (see «Not done»).

## Not done here (recorded)
- ⚠️ **The bell keeps its 30 s poll** (read through `pollMs`). Notifications of a task, an approval, a reward, a document are rows written by `src/server/notify.ts` and the services that call it, and none of them sends a frame, so the bell cannot be live without the poll. It does refresh on `message.created` and `ticket.changed` frames now (a mention or a private message shows within a second). 🔧 A `notification.created` frame sent from `notify.ts` (a file outside this package) would let the poll become the five-minute net like the other badges.
- ⚠️ **The spaces' overdue count has no frame**: a task writes none. It refreshes on every navigation, on the unread frames, and at the latest on the five-minute net, so it can be up to five minutes old while the person sits on one page. 🔧 A task event on the live line would make it live.
- The hub's "one query" is a lookup of at most four queries (the conversation with its members, then the people's company rows, space rows, team rows and the DM's pair, together). A single SQL statement would have copied the policy's rule into SQL; the batch calls the policy's own function instead.
- **C1.2 sync gaps, recorded again, not closed:** (1) deleting an empty topic emits a space-only event — naming the conversation needs an emit change in `topics.ts` (not this package); (2) removing a person from a space where they have no row in a topic, and offboarding or suspending, emit no event that names the conversation — that is in `sync.ts` / `accounts.ts`; (3) a client that already read past a lower id that commits late would miss it — C1.4's `scheduleSync` should also resync on a frame whose id is not above its cursor (every frame still carries its id and the page still dedupes nothing).
- A follower asks nothing of the server on its own, so a person with many tabs and a dead leader tab (frozen, not closed) has the 15 s polling until that tab wakes or closes; it does not steal the lock.
- `/api/rt` still registers the client before its per-client replay, so an event can arrive both live and replayed (F0.R's open item): the page still does not dedupe by id.
- A person's `presence.changed` nudge makes every dock of the company ask `/api/presence` once (folded to one request per second); fine at a team's size, a larger company would want the nudge scoped by space.
