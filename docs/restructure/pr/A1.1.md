# A1.1 — «قوالب»: permission templates (switch `permissionTemplates`, OFF)

Branch `feat/permission-templates`. No schema change, no migration: the templates live in `Organization.settings.roleTemplates` (existing JSON).

## Definition of Done
- What must work (switch ON):
  - Data: `Organization.settings.roleTemplates = [{ id, name, rank, caps[], revoked[] }]`, at most 30. `caps` are extras the rank does not give,
    `revoked` are boxes the rank gives that the template switches off. A company that never saved any gets the seven starters («مدير مساحة», «موظف»,
    «مبيعات وتأكيد طلبات», «ميديا باير», «مونتاج وتصميم», «محاسبة», «مشاهدة فقط»); the first save makes them its own editable copies.
  - Applying a template to a person (`POST /api/organizations/[id]/role-templates/[templateId]/apply { userId }`) goes THROUGH `updateAccount`
    (`accounts.ts`): the same rank rules (strictly below the applier's rank), nobody grants what they do not hold, the account's own audit row and
    `membership.changed`, plus the audit row `org.template_applied` (templateId, name, rank, the two lists written).
  - The person's sheet shows «القالب: X» and «معدّل» once the rank or either list was changed by hand afterwards (the roster reads the newest
    `org.template_applied` row back and compares it with what the person has now).
  - The create-account sheet gets «القالب» (above the rank, which then comes from the template) and keeps «المساحات»: `POST .../members` accepts `templateId`.
  - SECURITY: a template can never give more than the applier holds (an extra he lacks is `403 ptemplates.exceeds_applier`, nothing written, no audit);
    a GUEST template does not exist (refused at save, dropped at read, never applied); a guest, a partner, an owner and oneself are never the target;
    `archive.view` is in no template (refused at save, dropped at read and at apply); the rank defaults the applier lacks start switched off even when
    the rank stays the same.
  - Saving does not lose a concurrent save silently: `GET` returns a `rev` (a hash of the list), `PUT` must send it back; under the row lock of the company
    an older copy is `409 ptemplates.stale`. Only the `roleTemplates` key is replaced.
  - Who: `users.manage` lists and applies (the existing door `requireAccountsActor`); only the OWNER rank edits the list.
- Switch OFF (as merged): the three routes answer 404 `features.off`, an account with a `templateId` is refused before anything is made, the roster carries no
  `template`, the sheets draw nothing new; accounts are made and edited exactly as before.
- Tests: `tests/permission-templates.test.ts` (new, 26: the pure rules, the starters, the switch, the pieces of the screens) · `tests/db/permission-templates.test.ts`
  (new, 23: switch off, who lists and edits, the save and its race, apply, the applier's limits, rank rules, guest/partner/owner/self targets, archive.view,
  create with a template and spaces, audit rows). Must stay green and were run: `tests/db/partners`, `accounts-create/list/matrix/review/status/update`, `org-members`,
  `members-ui-api`, `members-ui-pages`, `feature-switches-new`, `active-capabilities`, `archive-capability`, `capability-guard`, `section-permissions*` (5),
  `section-grid-switch`, `pages-access-capabilities`, `search-capabilities`, `guest-internal-pages`, `guest-directory`, `spaces-members` and, unit, `members-ui-*`,
  `org-members-logic`, `policy-capabilities`, `section-grid*`, `nav-places*`, `pages-access*`, `workspace-role`, `active-membership-guard`, `features`, `control-panel`,
  `encoding-hygiene`, `arabic-ui`, `no-hardcoded-colors`, `repo-hygiene`, `guest-policy`.
- Edge cases that matter: two saves at the same moment (one 200, one 409); an entry written around the checks (a guest template, `archive.view`) is distrusted on read;
  a template of another company and a person of another company are «not found»; an offboarded person is frozen; a partner whose `users.manage` was taken away cannot apply.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none) · a feature removed instead of marked (none).

## Switches
`permissionTemplates` — default OFF — OFF: nothing is drawn, the routes answer «not found», accounts work as before — turn it back off in «الميزات»; nothing saved is touched.
⚠️ A template changes a person's rank and all their boxes at once. 🔧 The sheet says so next to the button, and every box can still be changed by hand afterwards (that shows «معدّل»).

## Decisions used
Built the plan's wording; two small choices of mine, both safe and easy to change: (1) only the OWNER rank edits the list («the owner names and edits them»), an ADMIN with `users.manage` lists and applies;
(2) a template is never applied to a GUEST (promoting a guest to staff is its own deliberate step), nor to an owner or partner.
