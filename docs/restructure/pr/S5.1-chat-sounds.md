# S5.1 — «أصوات الرسائل بكل المحادثات» (new switch `chatSounds`, OFF by default; no schema change, no migration)

The owner (2026-11-21, Levantine): there is no sound in the chats («بالسايات»), the ringtone is bad, he wants Telegram-like behaviour. This package adds a short message tone for a message in ANY conversation the person reads and has not muted, five original tones drawn by the browser, a volume, a try button, and a per-topic «كل الرسائل / الذكر بس».

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **A tone for a message in any unmuted conversation, not only the open one** (`src/components/chat/chime/chat-sounds-host.tsx`, mounted by `src/app/(app)/layout.tsx` beside `<CallRing />` only while `chatSounds` is on). A realtime frame carries ids only, so the listener asks the normal API: `GET /api/chat/conversations/[id]/header` (kind, `me.muted`, `me.oversight`; kept 15 s, cleared by a `conversation.changed` / `membership.changed` frame) and `GET …/messages?limit=8` (the message presented for me: `mine`, `silent`, `mentions`, `kind`). Nothing is sent to the server.
  2. **The rules are pure functions** (`src/lib/chat/chime/decide.ts`, pinned by tests): never my own message, a system line, a deleted one or one sent «ابعت بدون صوت»; never in a muted chat (a mention included, the same rule as `src/server/chat/notify.ts`); never for a reader in silent oversight; every private message sounds; a message in a topic notifies its members, with a per-topic choice (below); a mention (or «@الكل») plays the tone twice; a message older than 30 s (a replay after a reconnect) is silent; never while the tab is hidden (the server sends the push to a person who is not looking, and it has its own sound); the conversation that is open and visible adds nothing (it makes its own arrival sound).
  3. **Never two sounds within 600 ms** (`CHIME_GAP_MS`, enforced in `player.ts`; the person's own «جرّب الصوت» bypasses it).
  4. **Five original tones, no sound file** (`src/lib/chat/chime/tones.ts`, pure data: notes, overtones, a low-pass cutoff; `player.ts` draws them with oscillators, a filter and an exponential envelope, 150-450 ms, the volume as one master gain): «فقاعة» (the default: a soft two-note pop), «جرس» (a glassy bell), «ماريمبا», «نقرتين» (low and warm), «زقزقة» (a quick rising chirp). A test pins exactly five, unique ids, an Arabic label each, durations, and that no `.mp3/.wav/.ogg` or `new Audio(` is in the tone code. Nothing was downloaded; nothing is added to the bundle; no licence or credit line.
  5. **«الإعدادات ← الإشعارات»** (`src/components/settings-me/chat-sound-block.tsx`, mounted in `notifications-section.tsx`): the five tones as radios (choosing one plays it), a volume slider (0-100%, default 50%), and the button «جرّب الصوت» (the chosen tone at the chosen volume). The personal page needs no company switch.
  6. **Per device, in `localStorage` key `zk-chat-sound`** (`src/lib/chat/chime/prefs.ts`: pure `parseChatSoundPrefs` / `loadChatSoundPrefs` / `saveChatSoundPrefs`, tolerant of rubbish, the same pattern as `src/lib/nav/tab-wheel.ts`). The master switch «أصوات الدردشة» is the one that already exists in the same section (`zk.chat.sounds`, `src/lib/chat/sounds.ts`): there is ONE master switch, not two; the listener obeys it.
  7. **iPhone / Safari:** `primeChime()` resumes one `AudioContext` and plays one silent sample from the first gesture (`touchstart`, `pointerdown`, then `touchend` / `click` / `keydown`, after which the listeners go), the same approach as `src/lib/nav/wheel-tick.ts`. Before that nothing plays and nothing throws.
  8. **Per-topic «كل الرسائل / الذكر بس»** is CLIENT-SIDE ONLY (`topics` inside `zk-chat-sound`; only the «الذكر بس» exceptions are stored, at most 200). Reason: `ConversationMember` has only `mutedUntil` (no notify-level field), and a schema change for a sound is not worth it. It is chosen in the info panel of a topic (`info-sound.tsx`, mounted under «كتم الإشعارات» in `info-panel.tsx`), drawn only while the switch is on, never for a private chat, «الرسائل المحفوظة» or a reader in oversight.
  9. **The open conversation's own arrival sound** (`playChatSound` in `src/lib/chat/sounds.ts`) plays the chosen tone while the switch is on, and the old sound file exactly as before while it is off (the old tests are unchanged and green). The send sound is untouched.
  10. **The switch** `chatSounds` (group «الدردشة», OFF by default; name, hint with the ⚠️ / 🔧 notes in `ar.chat-sounds.json`; registered in `tests/features.test.ts` and `tests/db/features.test.ts` the way `chatOneList` / `meetingMinutes` were). Off: no listener is mounted, the open conversation sounds as before, nothing changes.
- The tests: `tests/chat-sounds-everywhere.test.ts` (tones, prefs, rules, throttle, the player against a recording fake `AudioContext`, the switch, wiring and no-hex guards, renders), `tests/chat-sounds-listener.test.ts` (the listener end to end with a fake line and a fake server: 10 cases), plus the updated switch lists in `tests/features.test.ts` / `tests/db/features.test.ts`.
- Edge cases that matter: a muted chat stays silent even for a mention; a hidden tab makes no request at all; the same frame twice sounds once; a replayed old message is silent; a topic on «الذكر بس» sounds for a mention and for «@الكل»; a private chat ignores the topic choice; a browser with no audio or a still-asleep one plays nothing and throws nothing.
- Blockers: none.

## Honest limits (tell the owner)
- Per-topic «الذكر بس» is remembered on THAT device only (not on his phone and laptop together). A server field would need a schema change; say so if he wants it synced.
- Each message costs the browser two small requests (the conversation's header, kept 15 s, and the last messages) unless the tab is hidden, the master switch is off or it is the open conversation: that is the price of knowing who wrote it and whether the chat is muted without changing the server. A very busy company would feel it; the server could carry «mine / muted / mentions» on the frame later.
- Two windows of the same browser that are both visible each play the tone (the 600 ms gap is per window). A hidden window plays nothing.
- A topic muted from another device takes up to 15 s (or the next `conversation.changed` frame) to be known here.
- The tones were invented by listening to nothing: they are drawn from note lists and have only been checked against a recording fake `AudioContext`, never heard. Taste (loudness, which one is the default) is for the owner's ears.
