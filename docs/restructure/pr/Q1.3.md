# Q1.3 — «/» in the composer and the CARD message

## Definition of Done
- What must work: (1) a new message kind `CARD` (additive: `ChatMessage.card JSONB NULL`, `ChatMessage.viaApp TEXT NULL`) that stores ONLY
  `{ type: "product", cardId }` and a readable line WITHOUT a price («كرت منتج: حراك»); (2) `GET /api/chat/recall?q=&kind=product|reply` and
  `POST /api/chat/conversations/[id]/recall`: staff who may read the sheet, the sheet approved and not archived, and then the ordinary send path
  (may post here, the send limiter) — an unapproved sheet or a person who may not read it gets «not found», a guest gets nothing; (3) what is
  sent is always the APPROVED version's name, even from an editor; (4) a card cannot be edited; a forward keeps it and is resolved again for its
  new readers; (5) each reader's screen asks for the card (`/api/knowledge/[id]`), so a guest never gets a price and a person outside the knowledge
  space sees the line only; (6) the composer: typing «/» (or «+» → «من المنتجات») opens the menu; «/منتج حراك » and «/رد توصيل » open their list
  searched; «منتج» sends the card, «رد» INSERTS the approved answer into the box (it never sends by itself); (7) the switch `quickRecall`, OFF,
  needs `knowledgeCards`.
- Tests: `tests/db/quick-recall.test.ts` (11), `tests/recall-client.test.ts` (4); must stay green: the chat tests, `tests/db/cards.test.ts`
  (`sendCard` is not touched), the whole suite.
- Edge cases that matter: the public send schema has no `card` field (only the server's own callers can make a CARD message); a card stands alone
  (no files, voice or poll); a word still being typed («/منت») opens nothing.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none).
