# S5.8 — «رقم الانتظار لكل مساحة» على قائمة الموبايل

B4 made each space its own account and wrote «the other spaces show only as numbers on the space dropdown», but the drawer showed only unread and overdue. This adds the third number: what waits for THIS person's decision in each of his OTHER spaces (the same thing «بانتظار قرارك» on «اليوم» and «بانتظاري» on «الطلبات» count), and one small dot on the header of the space he is in when the other spaces hold something.

## جرّب
1. شغّل «رقم الانتظار لكل مساحة» من «الميزات» (بتحتاج «صندوق الطلبات» و«القائمة الجديدة» شغّالتين).
2. خلّي حدا يبعتلك طلب موافقة بمساحة تانية غير اللي إنت فيها، أو يطلب منك تاخد مهمة هناك.
3. افتح القائمة على الموبايل: جنب المساحة التانية بيطلع رقم برتقالي صغير فيه عدد اللي بانتظار قرارك فيها، ونقطة برتقالية صغيرة على اسم المساحة اللي إنت فيها.
4. اضغط اسم المساحة: بتنفتح القائمة وبتشوف الرقم جنب كل مساحة. وافق على الطلب من هناك: الرقم بينزل أو بيختفي (بعد ما تتنقّل لصفحة، أو بعد خمس دقايق).
5. طفّي الميزة: بيختفي الرقم والنقطة والقائمة بترجع متل ما كانت.

## What changed
- **Switch** `spaceBadges` (`src/lib/features.ts`): group «المهام والشغل», `dependsOn` `workRequests` and `navV2`, OFF by default, name and hint in `ar.features.json` (⚠️ and 🔧 like the other switches). Registered in `tests/features.test.ts` and in `tests/db/features.test.ts` (the default-off list).
- **ONE source** (`src/server/services/work-requests.ts`): `waitingBySpace(actor, spaceIds)`. It reads the kinds of «بانتظاري» with the lists' OWN filters, which were pulled out of the sections into `assignmentsWhere`, `cardsWhere`, `sheetsWhere` (the sections now use them too, so the page, the number on «الشغل» and the badges cannot disagree) and `waitingCountBySpace` in `approvals.ts` (the old `waitingCount` is now the sum of it: same SQL, now `GROUP BY r."workspaceId"`). Each kind is read ONCE for all the spaces (approvals: one grouped SQL; cards: one `groupBy`; assignment requests: one read of the pending ones addressed to the person; sheets: one count in the knowledge space). No pass per space, no N+1.
- **Who gets a number**: only the spaces the person works in (the inbox's own `workSpacesOf`, so a space he is not in is never counted or named); a guest and an external partner get nothing (no key); the OWNER gets one for each space (the drawer already lists every space for him).
- **Route**: no new route. `GET /api/workspaces` (the ONE query the drawer already reads, `useSpaces`) carries `waiting` on each space of the company the person works in, only while `spaceBadges` and `workRequests` are on; 0 where nothing waits; the key is left out when the switches are off, for a guest, and when the count fails (logged `space_badges.count_failed`, the spaces and the other numbers still answer). With the switch off the answer is byte-for-byte what it was.
- **UI** (`src/components/shell/space-switcher.tsx`): `CountPills` takes `waiting` and draws a small badge (hourglass and the number, `bg-warn`) beside each OTHER space in the drawer's list (the space he is in draws none: its number is on «الشغل»); `SpaceDropdown` draws one small dot (`bg-warn`) on the closed header when the sum over the other spaces is above zero, with the sum for a screen reader. Zero draws nothing. Words in `ar.shell.json` (`navv2.space.waiting`, `navv2.space.waiting_elsewhere`); colours only from tokens.

## Tests (written first)

- Changed on purpose: nothing pinned (the new key is absent unless the switch is on).

## Limits
- ⚠️ **Tickets are not in the number of another space**: departments have no space, so a ticket waits in none; they stay in the number on «الشغل» of the space he is in and in «الشركة». The badge for a space can therefore be lower than «الطلبات» of that space (by the tickets).
- ⚠️ The number is as fresh as the drawer's one query: it is read again after each page and when a message arrives, and anyway every five minutes. A decision taken elsewhere shows after the next page. 🔧 A live event on approvals would make it instant (not part of this).
- The dot and the badges are on the PHONE drawer only. The computer's space menu is unchanged (it keeps unread, and overdue in the new shell).
- No request-level cache was added: the repository has no per-request cache pattern (`perf/request-cache` does not exist), and the numbers ride on a request the shell already makes.
- Assignment requests are counted by reading the pending ones addressed to the person (a handful at most) instead of a `groupBy`, because Prisma cannot group by a field of a relation.
