## Definition of Done

All of it is in the address (`?view=&scope=&chip=&sort=&group=&space=&project=&stage=&q=&month=&task=`).
  - Scope **«أنا»** = assigned to me or co-assigned, across ALL my ACTIVE spaces (an archived space and a space that is not mine are not there), with a row of space chips; **«المساحة»** = the current space's visible tasks; **«مشروع»** = a picker of the projects I may see. In **«الشركة»** (no projects, D-N3) the screen still works: «المساحة» is not offered and asking for it shows «أنا» with a one-line note.
  - **Every read goes through `taskVisibleWhere`** (P0.2): a task hidden from the person is not listed, not counted (the chips' numbers), not found by the search, not opened in the panel (404), in any scope.
