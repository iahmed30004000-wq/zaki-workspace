# C3c.3 — Scheduled messages: «جدول الإرسال» (`chatScheduled`, ON)

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **Schema.** A SEPARATE new table `ScheduledChatMessage` (`id`, `orgId`, `conversationId`, `authorId`, `body`, `attachmentIds` JSON, `mentions` JSON, `replyToId?`, `silent`, `sendAt`, `status` PENDING|SENT|FAILED|CANCELLED, `messageId?`, `clientId` unique per author, `createdAt`) and its enum, one additive migration (`20261123101600_scheduled_chat_message`, a new table only: nothing existing is touched; `migrate diff` against the schema is empty, checked with `prisma migrate diff` from the previous schema). Two columns beyond the spec's list, both needed: `noPreview` (the ✕ on the link's card in the composer must survive until the send) and `failReason` (the list says why a row failed). Separate ON PURPOSE: a future-dated row inside `ChatMessage` would need a «not yet» filter in about sixty readers and one forgotten filter is a leak. Nothing of the table is read by any message reader: a unit test pins that only `src/server/chat/scheduled.ts` and the job name the table, and that only the three routes and the job import the service.
  2. **Only the author.** Every read and write of a row is `authorId = the caller`. The list, the edit, the cancel and send-now answer 404 for another person's row (nobody learns it exists): another member, the guest, and the owner's silent eyes included. A privacy-matrix row (`tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts`, 4 conversation kinds x 9 personas): who may schedule equals who may post there (the silent eye who has not joined cannot), a refused person leaves no row and learns nothing, no message reader (the window, the list, the space list, the search, the sync feed, the oversight list) ever carries the words of a waiting row, and nobody touches another's row.
  3. **The job.** `runChatScheduled(now)` (`src/server/jobs/chat-scheduled.ts`) is called FIRST in the scheduler's locked 30-second tick (`scheduler.ts`), so it runs in the app by default and in the compose `worker` with `JOBS_IN_WORKER=1`, under the same advisory lock. A due row is claimed with `SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and sent through the NORMAL `sendMessage` AS the author with the clientId `sch:<row id>`: `sendMessage` re-checks `canRead` and `canPost` at that moment. Idempotent three ways: the lock (two runs, or a run and «ابعت هلّق», cannot both hold a row), the clientId (a message with that clientId IS the row's message), and a «twin» check before any permission-dependent work (a crash between the send and the mark: the next run only marks the row, even if the person lost access meanwhile).
  4. **The permission re-check.** A person who lost access (left the space, the topic closed, the DM's other person gone, suspended from the company) sends nothing: the row is FAILED with the reason, no message exists, and the author gets a bell note (`chat_scheduled_failed`) that carries no word of the message, no conversation title and no link (they may have lost access to it). Attachments referenced by id must still be the author's, unattached and alive (the normal claim).
  5. **The UI.** «جدول الإرسال» on the send button's menu (right-click, long press) next to C3c.2's entry; a dialog with a date and a time read in the COMPANY's time zone (the list route answers it), at least a minute and at most 365 days; after scheduling the box is emptied like after a send and a status line says so; a calendar icon in the composer while the conversation has waiting (or failed) rows, which opens the list: when each goes out, its words, «ابعت هلّق» / «عدّل» (words and time) / «احذف»; a failed row shows why and can only be deleted. A silent-oversight reader cannot schedule (no icon, no entry, 403 on the route).
  6. **Switch** `chatScheduled`, default ON. OFF: every route answers 404, the menu entry and the icon are not drawn (the composer's list request gets the 404), the job does not select the company's rows (they wait untouched and go out when it is back; a row more than a day late is then FAILED, not sent), the data stays.
- Tests (new, all green): `tests/db/chat-scheduled.test.ts` (31), `tests/chat-scheduled-job.test.ts` (34, no database), the privacy-matrix rows (+5, in `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts`), one test in `tests/features.test.ts`.
- Edge cases that matter: a row scheduled for exactly a minute ahead and exactly 365 days ahead is accepted and a millisecond beyond either is refused; an edit holds the same window and cannot empty the message; a row sent or cancelled cannot be edited, cancelled (sent) or sent again; cancelling twice is fine; two requests with the same `clientId` are one row; the same `clientId` for another conversation is refused; a file that is another person's, already attached, held by another waiting row, or too old for the row's time is refused when scheduling; «ابعت هلّق» by a person who may no longer write is refused and leaves the row as it was; a waiting row is never purged, finished rows are removed after 30 days; another company's switch does not hold this company's rows; the author's send quota (30 a minute) used up leaves the row for the next tick instead of failing it.

## Decisions used (say if one should change)
- **A row more than a day late is not sent.** The app was down, or the switch was off for days: a message that old would be a surprise. It is FAILED and its author is told. (`MAX_LATE_MS`, one constant.)
- **The words of a sent or cancelled row are blanked** (they live on in the message; a cancelled draft is nobody's business). A FAILED row keeps them so the author can copy them.
- **A message with files must go out within 24 hours of the upload.** Chat uploads that no message claims are deleted after 24 hours (`purgeOrphanUploads`) and cannot be claimed after that (`claimUploads`); both are in `attachments.ts`, not this package. The dialog and the server refuse a later time for a message with files (`chat.sched.e.files_window`). Lifting it needs a change to `attachments.ts` (skip files a waiting row holds).
- **The message's clientId is `sch:<row id>`, not the row's own `clientId`** (that one only makes the row itself idempotent), so it can never collide with a client's own message id.
- **Send-now on a refused person is an error to the person, the row stays**; the job on a refused person marks FAILED and tells them. A rate-limited author is neither: the row waits for the next tick.
- **Finished rows are removed after 30 days** by the job (a waiting row never).
- **The silent eye** gets 403 on schedule (the send's own `canPost`), and a read-only 200 with an empty list is the most the list route ever shows him.
