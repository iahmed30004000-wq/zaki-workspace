# W1.6 — duplicates marked, with a way out: routine, campaign item, ticket, card → task

## Definition of Done
- What must work:
  1. **«الروتين اليومي»** shows ⚠️ «هاد مكرّر مع المهام المتكررة» and 🔧 «حوّل الروتين لمهام متكررة» to everybody who may open the page. ONE routine becomes ONE recurring task: a template task titled like the routine, held by the routine's assignee, in the «صندوق المهام» of the routine's space (a routine with no space — or one in «الشركة», which holds no project — goes to the ASSIGNEE's first STANDARD space), with a `TaskRecurrence` (mode «حسب الجدول») on the routine's weekdays made by the EXISTING `setRecurrence` (empty weekdays = DAILY). `POST /api/production/routines/[id]/convert`.
  2. **The routine is ARCHIVED, never deleted** (`active = false`; its row and its `RoutineLog` rows stay). On a day it was ticked, the checklist still lists it (`archived: true`, marked «مؤرشف», read-only); the manager's list shows «حوّلتها لمهمة» with the task's link. **Idempotent:** a second (or simultaneous) click makes no second task and no second series (201 when made, 200 + `created:false` after); a conversion that stopped before the series was made is finished by the next click, on the same task.
  3. **Campaign items:** additive `CampaignItem.taskId`. «اعمل منه مهمة» (`POST /api/production/campaign-items/[id]/task`) makes the task through `createTask` in the person's inbox (the campaign's department space when they are in it), due on the item's day, and links both ways (the item holds `taskId`; the task shows its source). The campaign detail returns each item with `task` (named or hidden) and no raw `taskId`. An item of kind «مهمة» carries ⚠️ + 🔧.
  4. **Tickets:** additive `Task.sourceTicketId`. «حوّل لمهمة» (`POST /api/tickets/[id]/task`) in the ticket's window; the ticket's detail returns `task`. Only a READER of the ticket may convert it (a stranger: 404), never a guest (403).
  5. **Cards:** additive `Task.sourceCardId`. The card's links read the stored column besides the audit log; a task found only by the log has its column filled when the links are read (see "Not done").
  6. **PRIVACY.** A task made from a ticket/campaign item follows P0.2/P0.3: held by somebody → «المعنيين بس» when the company's default is on; a ticket that came from a private conversation (DM, group, restricted topic, «مقطع») → «المعنيين بس», and `visibility: "SPACE"` needs `confirmPublic` ON THE SERVER (422 `work.confirm_public_required`). **Publishing such a task later through `updateTask` also needs it** (the task remembers its ticket: `holdsPrivateTalk` in `tasks.ts`; before, only a task with `sourceMessageId` was checked). A ticket/campaign reader who may not open the task sees only `{ state: "hidden" }`: never its title, key or id (the ticket's detail, the campaign's detail, the task-source door). A task shows its source through `GET /api/production/task-source/[taskId]` (behind the task's own door; named only to a reader of the source; an external partner gets an empty list).
- Tests: `tests/db/work-duplicates.test.ts` (new, 33: A routine 12, B campaign item 6, C ticket 7, D card 2, E source 3, F banners 3), `tests/db/privacy-matrix.test.ts` (+3: the task-source door as one more surface of the owner's table).
- Edge cases that matter: routine with no space / in «الشركة»; the assignee not in the space or not active; an owner who holds no seat in the space (a series acts as the person who set it); `recurringTasks` off; a member without the production box; a conversion that stopped half way; two clicks at once; a task deleted afterwards (the FK clears the link, the item/ticket can be converted again); a ticket from a private chat; a reader who may not open the task; a campaign owner outside the department; another company's rows; a guest.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none: three nullable columns, three indexes, three `ON DELETE SET NULL` foreign keys, no data moved, no unique index).

## Decisions used
- **D-W2 is PENDING** (folding Ticket / CampaignItem / Routine into Task types for good): NOT built. This step links and marks only; nothing is copied or dropped.
- A routine has no column for its task, and the plan lists none: the link is the audit line `routine.converted` (`meta.taskId`) and the archive is `active = false` (the field a «paused» routine already uses; a routine paused by hand is told apart by having no such line).
- The converting person is the creator of the task, and the holder is the routine's assignee. By RANK (task-assign.ts): an assignee ABOVE the person is ASKED, not assigned (the template then waits for their answer) — the existing rule, not bypassed. - A task from a ticket / campaign item is held by the person who converts it unless they name somebody (`assigneeId` in the body; `null` = nobody).
