# Q1.1 — The product sheet

## Definition of Done
- What must work: (1) `PRODUCT` and `REPLY` join the card kinds, but stay OUT of a space's ordinary cards (its menu, its list, every ordinary card
  route answers «not found» for them); (2) `Card.approvedVersion` (additive) and the serving rule: a reader who is not an editor is served only the
  last APPROVED version (the card itself when its version is the approved one, else that `CardRevision`) with the LIVE snapshot, and never knows a
  sheet nobody approved exists; (3) who writes: the knowledge space's managers and the people the owner named; who approves: the owner and the
  people he named (the D-Q4 default); an edit by an editor who may not approve waits in «قيد المراجعة» while the old version keeps being served,
  an edit by an approver of an approved sheet stays approved; (4) the data and its limits («بدون عجقة»: the line ≤ 120, a step ≤ 80, a text ≤ 400,
  an answer ≤ 300, chips ≤ 20 of ≤ 60, ≤ 8 sections, ≤ 30 questions) held by the server; (5) the templates by category (health, clothing, devices,
  other; the owner can override), no caution by any of them; (6) every copy text in one pure module (a section, an answer, the offer message from the
  live snapshot that REFUSES a stale or missing price, «انسخي الكل» that never carries the caution), the Arabic-folded question search; (7) the page
  `/knowledge`, the sheet `/knowledge/[cardId]`, the editor, the owner's settings, the third library tab «المنتجات» (staff only, behind the switch);
  (8) «اسألي مشرفتك» opens an instant ticket for the named approver (the owner when none); (9) «الصقي كرت قديم» (a pure parser; a price in an old card
  is never taken); (10) the sync makes a DRAFT sheet for each product that has none (what Q1.0 left); (11) each copy is one line in the card log.
- Tests (new): `tests/knowledge-cards.test.ts`, `tests/copy-text.test.ts`, `tests/old-card-parser.test.ts`, `tests/knowledge-ui.test.ts`,
  `tests/db/knowledge-sheets.test.ts` (25), `tests/db/oms-sync-drafts.test.ts` (7). Updated, on purpose: `tests/nav-places.test.ts` (the 27th tab),
  `tests/db/guest-internal-pages.test.ts` (the page of the new tab is rendered as every rank), `tests/cards-render.test.ts` (the ordinary menu offers
  its six kinds and NOT the two knowledge kinds), the features lists. Must stay green: `tests/db/cards.test.ts`, the whole suite.
- Edge cases that matter: a guest never comes in (404), not even into the space; the caution is sent to editors only; the price is the live
  `external`, never typed; a sheet deleted by an editor is never made again by the sync; two products with one code get no draft.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none).

## Decisions used

D-Q10 («لشو بينفع» is the problems the product is for) — built the default. D-Q12 (the selling price is for every staff reader, never a guest) —
built the default: the price is the live snapshot, shown to every staff reader of the sheet, and a guest never comes in. All three waiting for the owner.
