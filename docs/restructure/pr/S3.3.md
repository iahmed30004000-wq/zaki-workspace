# S3.3 — remove the duplicates of settings and the control panel, leave links (`settingsOneDoor`, OFF)

## Definition of Done
- What must work (all of it only while the switch `settingsOneDoor` AND `controlPanelV2` are on, `oneDoorOnFor` / `panelOneDoorOn`; OFF is today, byte for byte; no new switch, no schema, no migration):
  1. **Each thing has ONE home.** The feature switches (`/settings/me/features`), the company's name, the box's health and the system tokens (`/settings/me/company`), the AI connection (`/settings/me/integrations?part=ai`), the reports (`/owner?s=reports`) and the space's people editor (`/spaces/<id>/settings?tab=people`) are each drawn on exactly one page of everything the owner can open. S3.1 and S3.2 had already moved them; this package pins it with a walk that renders every page and counts, and fixes what still pointed elsewhere (items 2 to 4).
  2. **Links stay at home.** Inside the moved bodies the parts of «التكاملات» and the link to the switches are addresses of the settings frame (`panelSectionHref`), no longer `/owner?s=…` that redirects back into the frame.
  3. **Space people from ONE place.** The spaces' board of «إعدادات الشركة» is a list: each card has ONE gear to the space's own settings page (`SettingsGear`), where its name, its people, its archive and its delete live; the card's menu (rename, archive, delete) is gone while the door is on. `/spaces/current` and `/spaces` lead to the same pages (they did; pinned).
  4. **No second door to the panel.** The settings frame no longer draws the row «لوحة التحكم» for an OWNER or an ADMIN (their menu holds the place «لوحة التحكم» since S3.2), and an admin who holds nothing of the company's group has no group.
  5. **Theme and digits: one home each.** The theme (and «تلقائي حسب الجهاز») moves from «الدردشة» to the section `language`, which is called «المظهر واللغة» (the digits are there too). The chat section keeps a one-line link to it; the avatar menu loses its theme row and sheet. (The app bar's switch is P2.1's and stays.)
  6. **The guide only under «المساعدة».** The first-steps card has no step «افتح الدليل»; the theme's step leads to «المظهر واللغة». The avatar menu, the drawer, the rail and the side menu had no guide row already (S3.1).
  7. **One meaning of «الناس».** «الناس» is the company's people (the panel's section, the staff directory). The space's own list is «أعضاء المساحة» (the tab); the activity table of the people part is «نشاط الناس».
  8. **One name for the archive.** «الأرشيف» in the one navigation and as the section's title (not «الأرشيف والرقابة»); the section does not repeat it as a second title «أرشيف المحادثات».
  9. **Nothing is a dead end.** A walk follows every old address (`/settings`, `/analytics`, `/owner?v=reports`, `/owner?v=ai`, `/owner/assistant/settings`, the old panel sections of the settings, `/spaces`, `/spaces/current`) to the page that now holds it, and the target answers. The section guard of `src/server/services/control-panel.ts` is untouched: a lead, a member and a guest are refused the company's sections as before, an admin's pages are the panel's own.
- Tests: `tests/settings-dedupe.test.ts` (new, 23), `tests/db/settings-dedupe.test.ts` (new, 15).
- Edge cases that matter: an admin (no switches, no AI part, no company name: «not found» as before); a lead, a member, a guest; the door without the panel (the first-steps card keeps its guide step); the panel drawn without the door (keeps the panel's own addresses and «الأرشيف والرقابة»); the archive page under the OFF panel keeps its title.
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red.
