# Q1.6 — «لازم ينقرأ»

## Definition of Done
- What must work: (1) a moderator of a topic or a holder of «إعلانات للجميع» (`announce.send`, checked nowhere before) marks a TEXT message of a TOPIC as
  must-read — from the message menu, or by sending it so; a plain member's flag is refused; (2) each person the topic reaches (ACTIVE staff, not the author, not a
  guest, not somebody who left) sees «قرأت ✓» under it; tapping writes a ✅ reaction of the existing `ChatReaction` (no new table), tapping again takes it back;
  (3) the author and the moderators see «قرأ 7 من 10» and the names of who has not; everybody else only their own state; a guest nothing; (4) a job reminds the
  people who have not read after 3 working hours and again the next working morning, each once, never someone who has read or is not active, and gives the author
  the list of late readers once a day; (5) the switch `mustRead`, OFF.
- Tests: `tests/db/must-read.test.ts` (11); must stay green: the chat, reaction and private-work tests, the whole suite.
- Edge cases that matter: a direct message cannot be marked; a deleted message cannot; the owner who is not in a topic does not count among its readers; two
  reminders at most per message; the working hours are the ones of Q1.5 (`Organization.settings.workHours`).
- Blockers: CI red · an old test red · a secret exposed · a destructive migration (none).

## Independent review (read-only agent)
No leak and no privilege escalation (a plain member and a guest never get the count or the names; the send path refuses a plain member, a guest, a direct message, a card, a poll and a voice note). Seven findings, each real one fixed with a failing test first: the reminders counted from the day the message was written instead of the day it was marked (an old post marked in the morning was chased at once; now `ackMeta.markedAt`); the job was too heavy and could remind twice (the audience is now set-based and kept 30 seconds for the screens, the job checks first whether any step is due, writes the step down before sending it and runs one at a time); marking and acknowledging did not check that the person may READ the topic (now «not found»); the late list went out at midnight and on days off and repeated the names just reminded (now a working day, in a run that did not remind); a reminder kept the text of a deleted or edited message in the bell (now rewritten like any notice); the reaction bar could write the ✅ for anybody (now refused); a double tap on «قرأت ✓» could fail (now skipped as a duplicate).
